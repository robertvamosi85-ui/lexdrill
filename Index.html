The risk pyramid
The AI Act's core organizing principle — all obligations flow from here
🚫 Unacceptable risk — PROHIBITED (Art. 5)
Social scoring · subliminal manipulation · real-time biometric ID in public spaces (narrow LE exceptions) · predictive policing based solely on profiling · biometric categorisation of sensitive attributes · emotion inference in workplace/education · facial recognition DB scraping
⚠️ High risk — HEAVY OBLIGATIONS (Art. 6 + Annex III)
Employment screening · credit scoring · biometric categorisation · critical infrastructure · education · migration · justice & democracy · law enforcement
💬 Limited risk — TRANSPARENCY ONLY (Art. 50)
Chatbots · deepfakes · emotion recognition — must disclose AI nature; deployer bears obligation
✅ Minimal / no risk — UNREGULATED (voluntary codes)
Spam filters · AI in video games · most recommendation engines
🤖 GPAI models — SEPARATE REGIME (Title VIII, Art. 51–56)
All GPAI: technical documentation + training content summary. Systemic risk (≥10²⁵ FLOPs): adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity
Key actors defined (Art. 3)
Provider
Develops or places AI system on market — bears heaviest obligations
Deployer
Uses AI system under its own authority — secondary obligations; bears FRIA duty
Importer
Brings non-EU-provider system into the EU market
Distributor
Makes system available without modifying it — lightest obligations

Compliance timeline
12 July 2024 done
Official Journal publication
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 published. AI Office established within the Commission.
1 August 2024 done
Entry into force
Legal existence. Clock starts on all phase deadlines. Standards bodies begin work on harmonised standards.
2 February 2025 done
Phase 1 — Prohibitions + AI literacy
Art. 5 bans enforceable. AI literacy obligations (Art. 4) apply. All banned AI systems must be discontinued.
2 August 2025 done
Phase 2 — GPAI + governance
Title VIII obligations apply (Art. 51–56). Codes of practice finalised. Member States designate national competent authorities.
NOW — May 2026 you are here
Preparation for full application
AI omnibus political agreement reached (7 May 2026). High-risk system operators preparing conformity assessments and QMS documentation.
2 August 2026 3 months
Phase 3 — Full application (most obligations)
High-risk AI systems under Annex III fully regulated. Conformity assessments, EU database registration, QMS, post-market monitoring required. All financial penalties applicable.
2 August 2027
High-risk AI in regulated products
Extended deadline for Art. 6(1) systems embedded in Annex I products (medical devices, machinery, vehicles). AI omnibus may extend further for some categories.
2 August 2030
Legacy systems compliance
High-risk systems placed on market before Aug 2026 used by public authorities must comply. Large-scale EU IT systems (SIS, Eurodac) comply by 31 Dec 2030.
